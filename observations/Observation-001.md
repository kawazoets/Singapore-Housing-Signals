# Observation 001

## Why I Started Singapore Housing Signals

**Date:** 2026-07-30

Over the past two days, the Singapore Government announced two significant policy changes.

- Removal of the 15-month wait-out period for private property owners purchasing HDB resale flats.
- Revision of the requirements for maintaining the Real Estate Salesperson (RES) licence.

These announcements made me realize that I had been observing individual housing-related news, but not the government's overall policy direction.

Rather than focusing only on housing prices, I decided to build a framework to observe how Singapore manages its housing market through the interaction of policy, market momentum, housing supply, and monetary policy.

This project is not intended to predict property prices.

Its purpose is to observe policy signals, record changes over time, and develop a deeper understanding of Singapore's long-term housing strategy.
